### Text Formats
- **`.md`** - Markdown libretti with embedded images and formatting
- **`.txt`** - Plain text individual scene files

### Image Formats
- **`.png`** - High-resolution illustrations (1024x1536 typically)
- 3-4 MB per image average

### Audio Formats
- **`.mp3`** - Compressed narration and music (128-320 kbps)
- **`.wav`** - Uncompressed full recordings

### Generate a New Opera
